Report something

If a post, photo, comment, story or account breaks our rules, tap the ⋯ menu on it and choose Report. You can also press and hold a message to report it. We review every report, and content involving anyone under 18 is removed immediately.

Block someone

Tap ⋯ on their post or profile and choose Block. They won't be able to see your content or message you, and they aren't told. To undo it, go to Profile → Settings → Blocked users.

Delete your account

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Your account is hidden immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days. Signing back in during those 30 days cancels the deletion.

Why can't I check in or report a line?

Both require you to be within 125 metres of the venue. This keeps wait times honest — it stops people reporting on places they aren't at. Make sure location access is turned on for The Move in your phone's Settings.

Wait times look wrong

Wait times come from other users and may be out of date. If a report looks false, report it from the ⋯ menu.
