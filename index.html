Privacy Policy — The Move

Last updated: 16 September 2026

The Move is operated by The Move.app LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address, name, username, and — if you add them — a profile photo and bio. Email is used to sign you in and to contact you about your account.

Location. When you check in at a venue or report a line, we ask for your device location and check that you are within 125 metres of that venue. This is what keeps wait times honest — it stops people reporting on places they are not at.

We only request location while you are using the app. We do not track you in the background.

The coordinates attached to a check-in or report are deleted automatically within a short period. We keep the fact that you were at a venue; we do not keep a history of where your phone has been.

Photos. When you report a line, the app takes a photo of the line and, optionally, a front-facing photo of you. Photos you post to stories are also stored.

Content you create. Posts, comments, reactions, direct messages, check-ins, and line reports.

Friends and connections. Who you are friends with, friend requests, and your visibility settings.

Device information for notifications. A push notification token, so we can send you alerts you have opted into. You control which notifications you receive in Settings.

Usage analytics. We record which screens you open and which actions you take, to understand how the app is used. This is tied to your account.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, your background location, your browsing outside the app, or payment information. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase — stores our database, files, and handles sign-in
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device
  • Mixpanel — usage analytics
  • Google — we use Google's Places service to fetch venue photos and addresses. This does not involve your personal data.
  • Resend — sends account and waitlist emails

Each of these acts on our instructions. We do not sell or rent your information.

All of these providers are United States companies, and your data is stored on servers in the United States. We do not transfer your information to companies outside the United States.

If we ever add a feature that sends your information to an artificial intelligence service, we will ask your permission first and explain what is being shared.

What other people can see

Your profile — name, username, photo, and bio — is visible to other users.

Your posts, line reports, and stories are visible according to where you post them.

Your location at a venue is hidden by default. It is only shown to friends if you turn friend visibility on, and you can hide from specific friends or use Masquerade mode to disappear entirely.

Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Profile → Delete Account.

Your account is deactivated immediately and you become invisible to everyone. After 30 days it is permanently deleted, along with your posts, photos, line reports, check-ins, friendships, and points. Signing in during those 30 days cancels the deletion.

One thing survives. Messages you sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as coming from a deleted user. We do this so the other person's conversation still makes sense. Your name, photo, and username are removed from them.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your information. Most of this is available in the app; for anything else, contact us at the address below.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights — including the right to a copy of your data, and the right to object to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond.

Children

The Move is not intended for anyone under 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encryption in transit and access controls on our database. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
